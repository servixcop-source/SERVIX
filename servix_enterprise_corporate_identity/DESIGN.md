## Brand & Style

This design system defines an authoritative, enterprise-grade digital ecosystem for integrated facility management, industrial hygiene, and smart asset preservation across Saudi Arabia. The aesthetic balances high-trust institutional reliability with precision environmental technology.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Institutional Authority:** Grounded, unshakeable, enterprise-ready. Conveys rigorous SLA adherence, industrial-scale capability, and operational resilience.
- **Hygiene & Sustainability Precision:** Pristine, structured, active. Reflects environmental compliance, clean energy protocols, and advanced facility maintenance.
- **Technological Mastery:** Data-driven, proactive, modern. Combines field operations with IoT asset tracking and digital work-order telemetry.

### Visual Style Direction
The aesthetic merges **Corporate / Modern** engineering with **Tactile Depth and Precision Edge**. Surface layers remain clean with crisp white cards (`#FFFFFF`) elevated over cool slate wash canvases (`#F8FAFC`). Depth is created through architectural ambient shadows tinted with deep navy undertones rather than flat grey tones. Layouts prioritize an **RTL-first architectural grid**, featuring bidirectional symmetry, generous breathing room, structured data cards, and purposeful emerald micro-accents.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional presence grounded in deep marine navy, balanced by restorative emerald teal that symbolizes precision hygiene, life-cycle care, and environmental compliance.

### Strategic Color Roles
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2438`):** The institutional anchor. Used for high-impact brand headers, primary call-to-action buttons, high-level navigation chrome, and definitive text anchors.
- **Secondary Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** The operational vitalizer. Denotes verified statuses, active maintenance health, environmental hygiene milestones, and success micro-indicators.
- **Tertiary Executive Blue (`#1E3A8A`):** The data accent. Used for analytical chart series, secondary informational states, interactive system links, and active state highlights.
- **Pristine Canvas (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`):** Neutral foundation ensuring industrial dashboards and corporate presentation layers remain clean and glare-free under intensive operational use.
- **Structural Neutral Slate (`#0F172A`, `#334155`, `#64748B`, `#E2E8F0`):** Provides sharp legibility gradients for enterprise tables, inspection logs, and technical specifications.

## Typography

The typographic architecture is built on IBM Plex Sans (paired seamlessly with IBM Plex Sans Arabic for localization). This pairing delivers an engineered, corporate tone that renders technical terminology, Arabic calligraphic structure, and Latin data with equal legibility.

### Hierarchy & Script Cohesion
- **Arabic Harmony:** Line heights for Arabic rendering must maintain a 1.45–1.6× baseline multiplier to accommodate ascenders, descenders, and tashkeel marks without vertical clipping.
- **Tabular Figures:** All analytical metrics, asset counts, area measurements ($m^2$), and currency values (SAR / ر.س) must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to ensure strict vertical data alignment across telemetry tables.
- **Optical Weighting:** Use bold weights (`700`) deliberately for hero statistics and top-tier section signifiers; maintain regular weights (`400`) for descriptive work-order scopes to avoid visual clutter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses an 8pt base grid within an RTL-first responsive structure. Grid alignments adapt dynamically to provide visual stability across desktop operations consoles and field mobile views.

### Layout Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12 Columns | 1280px–1600px Max-Width):** Utilizes `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem` to frame dense enterprise data tables, KPI metric grids, and master-detail work views.
- **Tablet (8 Columns | 768px–1024px):** Fluid transitions with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Side panels collapse into layered sheets.
- **Mobile (4 Columns | 320px–767px):** High-density single-column flow with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Action anchors are fixed to the lower viewport for field operation thumb reach.

### RTL Geometry Directives
- **Structural Mirroring:** Margins, padding vectors, and directional flows shift right-to-left naturally. Chevron cues, progress bars, step-forms, and telemetry timelines orient from right to left.
- **Numeric & Metric Anchors:** Telemetry graphs, phone numbers, and international codes retain LTR formatting embedded within the overarching RTL page context.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through subtle, cool-toned ambient shadows and pure white surface elevations placed over crisp slate backgrounds. This creates a clean, architectural sense of lift without relying on heavy borders or skeuomorphic gradients.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base application plane. Completely flat.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surface with a 1px perimeter border of `#E2E8F0` and an ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.05), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Popovers):** Elevated state during hover or selection: `0px 4px 12px 0px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.08), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Critical Action Dialogs):** High-priority focus layers: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.14), 0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.08)`. Backdrops use `rgba(15, 36, 56, 0.60)` with a subtle `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern curves that soften technical data while preserving industrial precision. Radii follow an intentional hierarchy:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for micro-components including badges, form input fields, tooltips, and table rows.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to standard dashboard cards, maintenance category selectors, service packages, and action panels.
- **Extra-Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / `24px`):** Applied to high-prominence hero banners, corporate summary cards, and primary modal containers.
- **Full Radius (`9999px`):** Exclusively reserved for status pill indicators, numerical avatars, and circular action icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Corporate Action):** Solid `#0F2438` fill, `#FFFFFF` typography, `0.5rem` radius, 12px 24px padding. Hover: `#1E3A8A`. Active: `#0A1826`.
- **Secondary (Hygiene / Care Milestone):** Solid `#059669` fill, `#FFFFFF` typography. Hover: `#047857`. Focus ring: 3px `#10B981` at 30% opacity.
- **Tertiary (Neutral Outline):** Transparent background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#0F2438` typography. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#0F2438` border.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Sanitized / Operational (Emerald):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border 1px solid `#A7F3D0`. Uses a leading 6px pulsing emerald indicator dot.
- **Pending / Scheduled Maintenance (Navy Tint):** Background `#EFF6FF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border 1px solid `#BFDBFE`.
- **Critical Attention (Amber/Rose):** Background `#FFF1F2`, text `#9F1239`, border 1px solid `#FECDD3`.

### Input Fields
- Built on a pure white `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px solid `#E2E8F0` border, `0.5rem` corner radius, and 10px 14px interior padding.
- Right-aligned RTL label text in `#334155` (`label-md`). 
- Focus state shifts border color to `#1E3A8A` with a distinct `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.12)` halo ring.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Pure `#FFFFFF` background with `1rem` border radius, surrounded by a subtle `#E2E8F0` border.
- Cards maintain distinct top-edge status indicators: an optional 3px top-border accent in `#059669` for active contracts, or `#1E3A8A` for enterprise asset reviews.
- Content structured with a fixed header, clear middle spec grid, and an action footer.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Crisp 20px squares/circles, 1.5px `#CBD5E1` unselected border.
- Checked state fills with `#059669` showing a crisp white icon indicator.

### Specialized Component: Facility KPI Gauge
- High-level telemetry card highlighting operational status, compliance percentages, and SLA metrics.
- Uses large tabular figures, accompanied by micro trend arrows (green for compliance increase, neutral for baseline stability) and subtle secondary slate labels.
